## How I Work

### Tool-Agnostic, Bootstrap-Aware
I design systems that work with what you have, not what I prefer.

**Typical Stack:**
- **Backend:** Supabase, PostgreSQL, API-first architectures
- **Automation:** Make, n8n, custom Edge Functions
- **AI:** Claude (Anthropic), GPT-4 (OpenAI), LLM-agnostic prompts
- **Data:** Airtable, Postgres, JSONB for flexible schemas
- **Analytics:** Privacy-first, human-in-loop dashboards

**Philosophy:** Use proven tools. Avoid over-engineering. Build for portability.

---

### Human-in-Loop by Design
My systems require human approval at critical gates:
- Candidates may enter automatically. Approval is always manual.
- AI extracts metadata. Humans approve into canon.
- Agents recommend partnerships. Humans decide outreach.
- Automation supports editorial judgment. Never replaces it.

**Why:** Credibility comes from human judgment, not algorithmic volume.

---

**Process:**

4. **Iterate** — Refine based on real usage (not theory)
5. **Scale** — Expand capacity without sacrificing quality

## Case Study: IDLW Credibility Spine

**Challenge:** Independent water integrity platform needed to scale curation without losing editorial credibility or burning out founder (Glen).

**Solution:**
- Built AI parsing system (Claude extracts metadata from Glen's free-form notes)
- Deployed two-table governance (candidate intake → canon approval)
- Implemented relationship tracking (contact capture, backlink scoring, outreach pipeline)
- Created community intelligence layer (NewPress monitoring, creator identification)
- Designed 4-week credibility sprint (measurable deliverables, Go/No-Go assessment)

**Outcome:**
- Glen adds stories in <2 minutes (down from 10+ minutes)
- AI parsing accuracy: 95%+ on clear notes, 80%+ on lazy notes
- Q2 outreach pipeline: 10-15 high-value creator targets identified
- Geographic credibility map: Stories span 15+ countries
- Relationship infrastructure: Ready for 30-email outreach campaign

**Timeline:** Core system operational in 2 weeks. Community intelligence layer in 1 week.
